TUBE STOPS

Guess the London Underground station in as few guesses as possible

TUBE STOPS

    How to play

    Every day there's a new mystery London Underground station. The map shows the tube lines that meet there. Can you name it?

    1. Count the lines: each line crossing the centre circle is a different tube line, so if you see 3 lines, the answer is a station on 3 tube lines.
    2. Start typing a station name and pick it from the list. You have 10 guesses.
    3. After 3 wrong guesses, the lines show their colours.
    4. After 7 wrong guesses, the next stop on each line is revealed.
    5. A yellow guess is within 5 stops of the answer, so you're getting close. Grey guesses are further away.
    6. Guess right and the station turns green. Come back tomorrow for a new puzzle!


    This game only includes London Underground stations, this means it does not include the Overground, Elizabeth Line, DLR, National Rail or Trams within London.
